About LAM Federal Solutions

LAM Federal Solutions is the government-contracting brand of LAVAR AUTO MATRIX LLC, a small business based in Atlantic City, New Jersey.

We serve federal, state, and local agencies and commercial clients as a prime contractor in two service lines — on-site IT and administrative services, and facility support services. Facility and field work is delivered through a managed bench of vetted local South Jersey trade partners, quality-inspected to contract standards.

Our model is simple: one accountable prime, local small-business expertise, and management that stays on the job until the work is verified complete.
